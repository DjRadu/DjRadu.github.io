My story and your party

I'm Radu, a DJ for weddings, christenings and private parties across Denmark.


I left Romania 15 years ago and built a life here. It took me a while to realize I could turn my passion for music and dancing into something I could give to others. It was the best decision I've ever made.


For me, every party is a story we build together. I talk with you first, I learn who your guests are, what this evening means to you all, and only then do I build a playlist that includes your wishes and what the people close to you want to hear. Then I adjust the energy based on what I feel in the room. Elegance and fun, good taste and energy, all in balance.


I play at weddings and christenings, at birthdays and corporate parties, with Romanian and international music for all the generations in the room. And because I dance myself to salsa, bachata, pop and house, I understand the dance floor from a dancer's perspective, not just from the booth. That helps me read the room better.


I speak Danish, English, Romanian and Italian, so no matter who your guests are, the atmosphere is in good hands.


The most important thing to know: I believe a successful party means you get to enjoy your own party while I take care of everything else.


Your Party. My Energy.


📍 I cover all of Denmark and Northern Germany in my own car. The rest of Europe can be arranged — write to me and we'll sort out travel and accommodation.
