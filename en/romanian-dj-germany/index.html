Romanian DJ in Germany

I play weddings, christenings and Romanian parties across northern Germany. I drive from Juelsminde, Denmark, with my own sound and lights, anywhere within 5–6 hours: Hamburg, Bremen, Bremerhaven, Kiel, Hanover, Berlin and the towns around them.

What you get

Music from home

Romanian folk, hora, sârbă, manele and Romanian pop, plus international hits for guests from other countries.

My own sound and lights

I bring equipment sized for your venue, the neon DJ stand and a smoke machine. Setup takes about two hours.

Until the very end

I stay for the whole party, sleep at a hotel nearby and drive home the next day.

We talk directly

Call or message me with the date, city and venue, and we agree on the rest together, including travel and the hotel.

Where I travel

Northern Germany: Hamburg (about 3 hours), Kiel, Lübeck, Flensburg, Bremen and Bremerhaven (about 4–5 hours), Oldenburg, Hanover, Rostock, Schwerin and Berlin (about 5–6 hours).

Within the same range: the northern Netherlands (Groningen, Friesland) and south-west Sweden (Malmö, Helsingborg, Lund).

Southern Germany (Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Munich) is not my usual area, as the drive is 9–10 hours or more. If you still want me there, write to me and we will see what is possible.

How it works

I leave in the morning, arrive a few hours early, set up sound and lights in about two hours and test everything before the guests arrive. I play the whole party, from the welcome and dinner to the hora and the last dance, then sleep at a hotel nearby and head home the next day.

That is how the christenings in Bremerhaven and at Schloss Sigrön near Wittenberge went: planned ahead, no rush and no stress for the hosts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which cities in Germany do you travel to?

Northern Germany, within 5–6 hours by car from Juelsminde: Hamburg, Bremen, Bremerhaven, Kiel, Lübeck, Hanover, Oldenburg, Rostock, Schwerin, Berlin and nearby towns.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the date, the city and the venue. Call or message me and we will agree on it, including travel and the hotel.

What music do you play at a Romanian wedding or christening?

Romanian folk from every region, hora, sârbă, manele, Romanian pop and international hits. I adapt to the room and take your favourite songs in advance.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes: sound, lights and the neon DJ stand. Setup takes about two hours.

How do I book?

Send me the date, city and venue, the type of event and the number of guests on WhatsApp or through the form. I will confirm availability and send you a quote.
