Salsa and bachata with Miguel Flores

Miguel has been teaching Danes to dance salsa since 1993. He is a dance instructor, choreographer and cultural speaker, and he gets even the shyest guests on the dance floor. 💃

My friend Miguel

Miguel is one of the reasons I became a DJ. Before I started on my own, I stood next to him at his Latin parties and helped with the music. He taught me that with salsa and bachata, what matters is the mood on the dance floor, not technical tricks. Now we work together: he gets the guests dancing, and I take care of the music.

Who is Miguel?

Miguel Flores came to Denmark from Peru in 1990. He went to a sports folk high school, learned Danish and built a new life here. In 1993 he opened Latin Danse Studio in Aarhus, one of the first salsa schools in the country, and he was one of the people who brought salsa to Denmark in the 90s.

Since then he has taught thousands of Danes, children, teenagers and adults, and organised Danish and European salsa championships. He gives talks on culture and integration at schools, upper secondary schools and municipalities, and at his own Latin parties he plays the music himself as DJ Miguel.

What Miguel offers

Dance classes

Salsa and bachata for children, teenagers and adults. No experience needed.

Dance lesson at your party

At a company party, birthday or wedding: Miguel teaches your guests the first steps, and the floor stays full for the rest of the night.

Dance therapy

Movement and music without any pressure to perform, for joy and well-being.

Talks and workshops

About life between Latin America and Denmark, culture and community. For schools, libraries, municipalities and companies.
