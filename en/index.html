About me

Radu Ghiorghiu

Romanian DJ based in Denmark

For me, every party is a story we build together.


Music isn't just a playlist. It's the heartbeat of your event — the energy that makes people dance, feel good, and connect. It's the atmosphere that stays in their memory for years.


At every event, I think about how to create that perfect moment: elegance and fun, good taste and pure energy, all in balance. I want every guest, regardless of age, to feel that they're exactly where they belong.


From weddings and christenings to birthdays and private events, I carefully choose every track and every moment — for you and your guests.


I speak fluent Danish, English, Romanian and Italian — so I can keep the night going no matter who your guests are. And because I dance salsa, bachata and merengue myself, I understand the dance floor from a dancer's perspective, not just a DJ's.


Your Party. My Energy.


📍 Covering Jutland & Funen (Fyn) — other areas of Denmark on request (travel negotiable)

Services

💍 Weddings

Music for every moment of the day — from the reception and dinner to the first dance and the party that follows. I read the room and adjust the playlist live.

🕯️ Christenings

A warm atmosphere for family and guests: quiet background music during the meal, and a lighter, joyful dance later on when the moment is right. Microphone included for toasts and well-wishes.

🎓 Confirmation Parties

The right music for a confirmation day, from the elegant family moments over lunch to the evening party with friends.

🎂 Birthdays

Private parties filled with energy and great music — from milestone birthdays to silver and golden wedding anniversaries, with music that works for every generation in the room.

🎄 Julefrokost / Christmas Parties

Your office or friends' Christmas party, with quiet music through dinner and rising energy all the way to the dance floor — the real rhythm of a Danish julefrokost.

🏢 Corporate events

Professional events, company parties and team-building evenings — including grand openings and inaugurations of a new office or location, where the right music matters as much as the presentation.

💃 Latin & Salsa Nights

Salsa, bachata, merengue — Latin music mixed by a DJ who's also a dancer, so I know exactly what keeps the floor full.
