Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment do you bring?

I bring my own sound and lighting system — JBL PartyBox 720 and Club 120 for sound, ADJ Fusion FX Bar 3 for lights — plus a microphone for speeches and for the toastmaster. The setup is scaled to the room and the number of guests.

When do you arrive, and how long does setup take?

I arrive at least two and a half hours before the event starts. Setup takes around two hours, so everything is tested and ready before the first guests walk in.

What do you need at the venue?

A power outlet near the DJ area and a bit of space for the equipment. We sort out the details beforehand, depending on the venue.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on how long the event runs, the location and the type of party. We settle it after a short conversation, once I know what you have in mind.

What happens if you fall ill?

I have a network of fellow DJs I can call on, both in the Romanian community in Denmark and in the Latin music scene. If something unexpected comes up, I find a solution together with you.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, especially during wedding season and around the Christmas party period. Sometimes I am free at short notice, so it is always worth asking.

Do you take requests from guests during the party?

Yes, gladly. I like it when people come up and ask for a song — it means they are enjoying themselves. I fit them in where they suit the evening best.
