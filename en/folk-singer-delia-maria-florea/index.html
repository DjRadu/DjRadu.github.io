How I met Delia

I heard Delia sing twice on New Year’s Eve, when I was there as a guest, not as the DJ. I was impressed by how confidently she sings and how she gets the whole room on its feet. That is how our idea started: when a party wants live music, I recommend Delia, and when someone is looking for a DJ, Delia recommends me.

Who is Delia?

Delia Maria Florea is a young Romanian folk singer with roots in the Iași region, living in Denmark. She sings folk music from Moldova: hora, bătute, table and drinking songs, live, at weddings, christenings and Romanian parties.

On 14 November 2026 she sang at the Lăsatul Secului party at Skave Hallen in Holstebro, together with Ion Paladi and Taraful Florin Mucea.

What Delia brings to your party

A live moment at christenings and weddings

A few songs sung live at the moment you choose: the entrance, the cake or midnight.

Hora and bătute

Romanian folk dance music from Moldova that gets everyone up from the table.

Table songs

Feel-good songs for the moments when the whole room sings together.
