Ursitoare fairies for a christening

Three fairies visit the child and wish them luck, health and a good life. It is the Romanian Ursitoare tradition, one of the most moving moments of a christening, and now you can have it in Denmark too. ✨
See the fairies Message Mascote Vesele

A fairy-tale moment

This year alone, parents have asked me twice if I know anyone who does Ursitoare. Now I do: Mascote Vesele from Sønderborg. The three fairies arrive in storybook dresses, give the child personal wishes and take photos with the parents and godparents.

A tradition from across the Balkans

The custom is best known in southern Romania, but it is close to the Bulgarian orisnitsi, the Serbian sudjaje and the Greek Moirai, who in folk belief visit the child on the third night. If your family shares the tradition, or you simply want a magical touch at the christening, the fairies fit right in.

What you get

30 minute show

The fairies' entrance, three personal wishes, a closing ritual, photos with parents and godparents, instant photos and a surprise.

60 minute show

Everything in the short show, plus the full ceremony, the Book of Destiny, a family moment and photos with the guests.

Church decoration

Mascote Vesele are based in Sønderborg. Travel is included within 50 km, and for longer distances you get the price before you confirm. The date is not reserved automatically: call or message them on WhatsApp to check it is free.
