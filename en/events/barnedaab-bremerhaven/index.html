Christening

Bremerhaven, Germany
← All events

A christening in Bremerhaven, northern Germany, with hora and Romanian folk dances until well after midnight. I drove about five hours from Juelsminde, set up sound, lights and the neon DJ stand in the hall, stayed at a hotel after the party and drove home the next day. In the evening I turned on the smoke machine, and the lights through the white smoke were a hit on the dance floor. Here are the entrance, the stage and a few moments from the hora.

Romanian DJ for weddings and christenings in Germany

I play weddings, christenings and Romanian parties across northern Germany: Bremerhaven, Bremen, Hamburg, Kiel, Hanover, Berlin and the towns around them. I also travel to the northern Netherlands and south-west Sweden, anywhere within a 5–6 hour drive.

I bring my own sound and lights, set up in about two hours and stay until the end, with Romanian folk music, hora, manele and party hits, whatever the room wants. If your party is further away, write to me and we will work it out.
