Birthday Party

Løsning, Hedensted
← All events

A colourful birthday party in Løsning near Hedensted, with a cactus bar, a balloon wall, a glitter wall and flower garlands around everyone's neck. I set up the sound, lights and neon sign in the hall in the morning and played until the party ended late in the evening. Here are the decorations, the setup and a few clips from behind the decks.

About Festlig og Balloner

The birthday host, Bernadette, runs her own company, Festlig og Balloner, which she started in Hedensted in August 2026. As the name says (“Festive and Balloons”), it is all about party decorations and balloons: balloon walls, photo backdrops and the kind of styling that turns an ordinary room into a party venue.

You could see it at the party. The pink balloon wall with the throne chair, the glitter wall with gold balloons, the cactus bar and the decorated tables made the hall something really special. If you need decorations and balloons for your next party, get in touch with her.
